About phuantimatter

Designed around progression, discovery, and growth.

The experience centers on collecting antimatter across many dimensions, gaining points, and using your progress to strengthen your matter world over time.

100 dimensions

Progress through a long journey of dimensions, each step bringing new goals and a bigger challenge.

Point-based growth

Earn points from your discoveries and use them to strengthen your world and unlock the next stage.

Level up your world

Every collection run contributes to a larger matter world that becomes more powerful as you progress.

Gameplay journey

  1. Start your run in the first dimension.
  2. Collect antimatter to gain points.
  3. Unlock higher dimensions and tougher levels.
  4. Invest your progress into your matter world.
